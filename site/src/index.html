How it works

A real QR matrix, packed into text.

Each QR module is expanded into a dense dot square, then every two-by-four region is encoded as one Unicode Braille character.

01

Encode

A standards-compliant QR matrix is generated with selectable error correction and a four-module quiet zone.

02

Supersample

Each module becomes a higher-resolution dot block, preserving enough visual density for camera readers.

03

Pack

The dots are losslessly mapped into Unicode Braille and rendered as a fixed, justified grid.
