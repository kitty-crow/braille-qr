About the project

Dense QR codes without image files.

Braille QR began as an experiment: could a genuine QR matrix be represented entirely as Unicode text and still remain scannable? Supersampling the modules and preserving the Braille cell geometry made it work.

Design principles

  • Generate a genuine QR matrix rather than QR-like art.
  • Keep the dot mapping lossless and strongly typed.
  • Preserve square modules across browsers and terminals.
  • Offer useful defaults with advanced controls available.

Technology

The CLI and web source use Bun and TypeScript. The QR encoder is provided by the established qrcode package, while the dot scaling and Unicode Braille packing are project code.

Privacy

The web generator runs locally in your browser. Text and tokens are not sent to a project server. Downloaded token pages are bearer credentials and should be handled as secrets.
